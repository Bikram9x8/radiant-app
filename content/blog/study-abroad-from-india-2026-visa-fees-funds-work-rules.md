Choosing a country to study in is usually about three money questions: **how much the student visa costs, how much money you must show, and whether you can work.** Below are the answers for Canada, the UK, Australia and Germany, taken only from official government pages. Every number has its source at the end, with the date we checked it.

> **Rupee amounts are estimates.** They use the official reference rates on the dates shown in the sources section. Your bank's rate will be different. Always check the official page before you apply, because fees and rules change often.

## Canada

- **Visa fee:** the study permit costs **CAD 150**, and biometrics cost **CAD 85** more.
- **Money to show:** for applications from **1 September 2026**, one student studying outside Quebec must show **CAD 23,448 a year** for living costs. This is **on top of** tuition and travel.
- **Work while studying:** up to **24 hours a week** off campus during regular terms. There's no limit during scheduled breaks, if you're eligible.
- **After graduation:** the **Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)** can be valid for **up to 3 years**. The length depends on your programme's level and length, and it can't be longer than your passport's validity.

## United Kingdom

- **Visa fee:** **£558** when you apply from outside the UK.
- **Health surcharge:** you also pay the **Immigration Health Surcharge**, **£776 a year** for students, when you apply.
- **Money to show:** **£1,529 a month** if you study in London, or **£1,171 a month** outside London, for **up to 9 months**. For 9 months, that's about ₹16.9 lakh (London) or ₹13 lakh (outside London).
- **English:** degree level and above needs **CEFR B2**; below degree level needs **B1**.
- **Work while studying:** **20 hours a week** in term time and full time outside term for degree-level students; **10 hours** below degree level.
- **After graduation:** the **Graduate visa** is **2 years** if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and **18 months** if you apply from 1 January 2027. PhD graduates get **3 years**.

## Australia

- **Visa fee:** the Student visa (subclass 500) costs **from AUD 2,500**. It went up from AUD 2,000 on 1 July 2026.
- **Money to show:** **AUD 29,710** in living costs for 12 months, plus 12 months of course fees and your travel costs.
- **English:** for tests taken on or after 7 August 2025, the minimum is **IELTS Academic 6.0**, **PTE Academic 47** or **TOEFL iBT 67**. The minimum is lower if your course includes English language study (ELICOS).
- **Work while studying:** up to **48 hours a fortnight** while your course is in session.
- **After graduation:** the **Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)**, Post-Higher Education Work stream, usually lets you stay **2 to 3 years**, depending on your qualification. It costs **from AUD 5,750** (≈ ₹3.6 lakh), and you must usually be **35 or under** when you apply.

## Germany

- **Visa fee:** a national (long-stay) visa costs **€75**. People with a scholarship from public funds may not have to pay.
- **Work while studying:** up to **140 full days or 280 half days a year**. A day of up to 4 hours counts as a half day.
- **Tuition in Baden-Württemberg:** students coming from **outside the EU** pay **€1,500 per semester** (≈ ₹1.5 lakh). The university decides exceptions.
- **Scholarship:** the DAAD **Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS)** programme pays **€992 a month** to graduates, plus insurance and a travel allowance. You need **at least two years' work experience**, and **India is on its country list**.

## Which country fits your budget?

- **Lowest visa fee:** Germany (€75), then Canada (CAD 150).
- **Highest visa fee:** Australia (AUD 2,500), then the UK (£558 plus the health surcharge).
- **Most work hours while studying:** Australia (48 hours a fortnight) and Canada (24 hours a week) allow more than the UK's 20 hours a week.
- **Longest post-study work, as listed here:** Canada (up to 3 years) and Australia (usually 2 to 3 years).

Remember that **tuition** is the biggest cost and is **set by each university** in Canada, the UK and Australia. Check your university's fees page.
